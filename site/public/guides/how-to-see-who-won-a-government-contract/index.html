How to see who won a government contract

Short answer: Every federal award on USAspending.gov names its recipient. Search by keyword or agency to see the winners for a type of work, or filter by recipient name to list everything a competitor has won.

Tool: USAspending Federal Awards | Run it on Apify | MCP: https://gouda-studios-agent-tools.vercel.app/mcp

Steps

  1. To find winners for a type of work, search by keywords and NAICS codes.
  2. To follow a competitor, put its name or UEI in recipientNames.
  3. Sort the results by amount or date in your sheet or database.

Example input

{
  "recipientNames": [
    "Booz Allen Hamilton"
  ],
  "maxResults": 200
}

FAQ

Can I see subcontractors?

This tool returns prime awards. Subaward data is published separately by USAspending.

Is the data free to use?

Yes. USAspending.gov data is public. You pay only per result returned by the tool.
