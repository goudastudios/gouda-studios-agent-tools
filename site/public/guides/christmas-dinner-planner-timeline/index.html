Christmas dinner planner: how to make a cooking timeline so everything is ready at once

Short answer: Pick your serving time first, then work backwards. For each dish write its cook time, rest time and oven temperature, subtract them from the serving time to get its start time, and sort the list by start time. Anything that needs the oven at a different temperature at the same time is a clash to fix before the day.

Start with the menu and the guest list

Write every dish under starters, mains, sides, desserts and drinks, and next to each guest note any allergies or diets and what they are bringing. This tells you how many portions you need and stops two people bringing the same dessert.

One shopping list, sorted by section

Go through each recipe and copy every ingredient onto a single list grouped by store section (produce, meat, dairy, bakery, pantry, drinks). Mark items that keep, like pantry goods and drinks, to buy early, and leave fresh produce for the last few days.

Build the timeline backwards

For each dish, list the minutes it needs to cook and to rest. Start time = Serving time minus Cook time minus Rest time. If you keep it in a spreadsheet with the serving time in B1 and minutes in columns C and D, the start time for row 3 is:

  • =$B$1-(C3+D3)/1440

Check the oven

Write each dish's oven temperature next to its start and end times. If two dishes need the oven at different temperatures in the same window, move one to the hob, slow cooker or microwave, or cook it earlier and reheat it while the roast rests. The resting time of a large roast is usually the best window for roasting vegetables and heating sides.

Food safety

Use a meat thermometer rather than the clock alone. The USDA says turkey and other poultry are safe at an internal temperature of 165 degrees F, measured in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost part of the thigh and the innermost part of the wing. Check the cooking time chart for your bird's weight and allow extra time if it is stuffed.

The day before

Most of the prep can happen on Christmas Eve: peel and chop vegetables, make sauces and desserts, set the table, and check that the bird has fully thawed. Leaving only cooking and carving for the day itself is what makes it feel easy.

FAQ

How far ahead should I plan Christmas dinner?

About two to three weeks for the menu, guest list and ordering a bird or meat, then a final shopping trip two or three days before.

How long does a turkey take to thaw in the fridge?

The USDA guideline is about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds, so start early for a large bird.
