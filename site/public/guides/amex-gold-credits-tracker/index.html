Amex Gold credits tracker: use the dining, Uber Cash, Resy and Dunkin' credits every month

The Amex Gold credits (as listed by American Express, November 2026)

Up to $424 a year in recurring credits. Amex changes the partner list from time to time, so check your card's benefits page and copy the current details into the sheet.

  • Dining credit: up to $10 a month in statement credits at Grubhub (including Seamless), Buffalo Wild Wings, Five Guys, The Cheesecake Factory and Wonder. Enrollment required.
  • Uber Cash: $10 a month after you add the Gold Card to your Uber account, for U.S. rides and Uber Eats orders. Unused Uber Cash does not roll over to the next month.
  • Resy credit: up to $50 from January through June and $50 from July through December at qualifying U.S. Resy restaurants. Enrollment required.
  • Dunkin' credit: up to $7 a month at U.S. Dunkin' locations. Enrollment required.
  • Annual fee: $325 for the basic card.

Columns

One row per credit:

  • Credit name
  • Value per period ($)
  • Cadence: Monthly or Semiannual
  • Enrolled or linked? (checkbox)
  • Used this period ($)
  • Left = Value minus Used
  • Next reset date
  • Days to reset = Next reset minus TODAY()
  • How to trigger it (merchant, app or link step)

Reset date formulas

  • Monthly (dining, Uber Cash, Dunkin'): =EOMONTH(TODAY(),0)+1
  • Semiannual Resy (Jan and Jul): =IF(MONTH(TODAY())<=6,DATE(YEAR(TODAY()),7,1),DATE(YEAR(TODAY())+1,1,1))

Alerts so nothing expires unused

Add two conditional formatting rules. Red when money is left and reset is close: =AND($F2>0,$H2<=5). Amber when a credit is not enrolled or linked yet: =$D2=FALSE. The monthly credits are small, so the usual loss is forgetting one for a few months in a row.

Make each credit automatic

Most people lose these credits through friction, not choice. Set the Gold Card as the default payment in Uber and Grubhub, put a recurring reminder near the end of each month for anything still unused, and plan one Resy dinner before June 30 and one before December 31.

Does the Gold Card earn its fee?

Total the credits you actually used over the cardmember year and only count spending you would have done anyway. If that number plus the value of the points you earn on dining and groceries is below $325, the card is costing you money.

FAQ

Do Amex Gold credits roll over?

No. The monthly dining, Uber Cash and Dunkin' credits reset each month, and each half of the Resy credit resets on January 1 and July 1. Unused amounts are lost.

Do I have to enroll?

Amex says the dining, Resy and Dunkin' credits require enrollment, and Uber Cash needs the card added to your Uber account. Do this once and check it off in the tracker.

Can I track my Gold and Platinum credits together?

Yes. Add a Card column and keep one row per credit across every card, then roll it up per card. The benefits tracker below already does this.
