Christmas party planner checklist: guest list, budget, menu and a week-by-week countdown

Short answer: Pick the date and headcount first, then set a total budget and divide it by guests to get a per-head number. Track invitations and RSVPs in one list, size food and drinks from the confirmed headcount, and work backwards from party day: invites 4 weeks out, menu and shopping list 2 weeks out, cleaning and prep the day before.

Start with three numbers

Everything else follows from these. Write them at the top of the plan.

  • Party date and start and end time
  • Expected headcount (invited times your usual acceptance rate)
  • Total budget, then budget per guest = total divided by headcount

Guest list and RSVP tracker

One row per guest or household:

  • Name
  • Invited on (date)
  • RSVP: Yes, No or Waiting
  • Number attending (plus ones and kids)
  • Dietary notes
  • Bringing anything?
  • Confirmed headcount: =SUMIF(C:C,"Yes",D:D)

Budget split

A simple starting split for a home party. Move money between lines to match what matters most to you.

  • Food: about half
  • Drinks: about a quarter to a third
  • Decorations, tableware and ice: the rest
  • Keep a small buffer for last-minute runs

Sizing food and drinks

For a drinks-and-appetizers evening party, plan roughly 10 to 15 small bites per guest over a few hours, and about one drink per guest per hour plus water and a non-alcoholic option. For a sit-down dinner, plan the main per guest and cut back on appetizers. Base the shopping list on the confirmed headcount, not the invite list.

Four week countdown

  • 4 weeks out: set date, budget and guest list, send invitations
  • 3 weeks out: choose the menu and any theme, order anything that ships
  • 2 weeks out: chase RSVPs, write the shopping list sorted by store section, plan the playlist
  • 1 week out: buy drinks and non-perishables, check chairs, glasses and serving dishes
  • 2 days out: buy fresh food, make anything that keeps
  • Day before: clean, set up decorations and the drinks station, prep ingredients
  • Party day: final cooking, ice, lights on 30 minutes before guests arrive

After the party

Note what ran out and what was left over next to each item on the shopping list. Next year that note is the most useful page in the plan.

FAQ

How far ahead should I send Christmas party invitations?

About 3 to 4 weeks ahead is a common choice in December, because calendars fill quickly. Send a reminder about a week before.

How much food do I need per guest for a holiday party?

For an evening drinks party with appetizers, about 10 to 15 small bites per guest is a common rule of thumb. Adjust up if the party runs through a mealtime.

How do I track RSVPs?

Keep one list with an RSVP column and a number attending column, then add up attending only where the RSVP is Yes. In Google Sheets or Excel, =SUMIF(C:C,"Yes",D:D) gives the confirmed headcount.
