How to find small business set-aside contracts

Short answer: Filter SAM.gov opportunities by set-aside code. The SAM.gov Contract Opportunities tool accepts codes like SBA, 8A, HZC, SDVOSBC and WOSB, plus your NAICS codes, and shows the days left to respond on each notice.

Tool: SAM.gov Contract Opportunities | Run it on Apify | MCP: https://gouda-studios-agent-tools.vercel.app/mcp

Steps

  1. Choose the set-aside codes that match your certifications.
  2. Add your NAICS codes and, if needed, states for the place of performance.
  3. Use minDaysUntilResponse to skip notices that close too soon to bid.
  4. Schedule it with onlyNew for a daily list of new set-aside opportunities.

Example input

{
  "setAsides": [
    "SBA",
    "8A"
  ],
  "naicsCodes": [
    "541512"
  ],
  "minDaysUntilResponse": 10,
  "maxResults": 100
}

FAQ

What does SBA mean as a set-aside?

A total small business set-aside, open only to firms that qualify as small under the NAICS size standard.

Can I search several set-aside types at once?

Yes. setAsides takes a list.
