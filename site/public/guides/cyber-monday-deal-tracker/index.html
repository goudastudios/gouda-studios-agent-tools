Cyber Monday deal tracker: log online deals against your gift budget before you buy

Short answer: Carry your gift wish list into Cyber Monday with a max price on every row. As online deals appear, log the sale price next to that max. An IF formula marks Buy only when the sale price is at or under your max; otherwise mark Skip. Total the Buy rows against your holiday gift budget so one good deal does not blow the plan.

Start from the same wish list as Black Friday

Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026, three days after Black Friday. Reuse the same person and gift idea rows so you do not re-shop for people you already covered.

  • A: Person
  • B: Gift idea
  • C: Max price you will pay
  • D: Site to watch (Amazon, Best Buy, brand store, etc.)
  • E: Link or SKU
  • F: Priority (Must / Nice / Skip if not on sale)
  • G: Already bought on Black Friday? (Yes / No)

Cyber Monday deal log columns

Fill these as deals appear. Keep them on the same row as the wish list item, or on a second tab keyed to Person + Gift idea.

  • H: Sale price
  • I: Shipping or tax estimate
  • J: All-in cost: =IF(H2="","",H2+IF(I2="",0,I2))
  • K: Under max?: =IF(J2="","",IF(J2<=C2,"Buy","Skip"))
  • L: Discount vs max %: =IF(OR(C2="",J2=""),"",ROUND((C2-J2)/C2*100,0))
  • M: Bought? (Yes / No / Waiting / Cart)
  • N: Order number or confirmation
  • O: Return-by date

Why all-in cost matters online

Cyber Monday deals are mostly shipped. A low sale price can still miss your max once shipping and tax are added. The All-in cost column keeps that honest before you click Buy.

Budget rollup for the weekend

Add a small summary so sale-weekend spending stays inside the gift budget.

  • Must-buy remaining: =COUNTIFS(F:F,"Must",G:G,"No",M:M,"<>Yes")
  • Spend if you take every Buy: =SUMIF(K:K,"Buy",J:J)
  • Already spent this weekend: =SUMIF(M:M,"Yes",J:J)
  • Gift budget left (budget in P1): =P1-SUMIF(M:M,"Yes",J:J)

A simple Cyber Monday buy rule

Only buy when Under max says Buy, Priority is Must or Nice, and Already bought on Black Friday is No. Percent-off banners and lightning timers are noise; the max price is the decision.

Cart and return discipline

Use Cart status for items you are still comparing across tabs. Set a return-by date the day the order confirms, because Cyber Monday return windows are often short and some marketplace sellers are final sale.

FAQ

Is there a free Cyber Monday deal tracker spreadsheet?

Yes. The columns and formulas on this page are a working tracker you can copy into Google Sheets or Excel. The Christmas gift tracker template below already includes per-person budgets, a gift list, a holiday budget and an expense log you can reuse for Cyber Monday.

Should I wait for Cyber Monday if Black Friday missed my max?

Yes for online-only items and many electronics. Keep the same max price. If Cyber Monday still misses it, skip the item rather than raising the max mid-weekend.

How do I avoid double-buying the same person a gift?

Filter or sort by the Already bought on Black Friday column and hide Yes rows before you shop. The Must-buy remaining count above also ignores people you already covered.
