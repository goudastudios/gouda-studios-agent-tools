Black Friday gift budget tracker: plan what to buy and what to skip before the sales

Short answer: Before Black Friday, write every person and gift idea with a max price you will pay. During the sales, log each deal next to that max price. Buy only when the sale price is at or under your max; otherwise leave it. A simple IF formula flags deals that still cost more than you planned.

Build the wish list first

Sales only help if you already know what you are shopping for. Make one row per gift idea before the weekend starts.

  • A: Person
  • B: Gift idea
  • C: Max price you will pay
  • D: Store or site to watch
  • E: Link or SKU
  • F: Priority (Must / Nice / Skip if not on sale)

Deal tracker columns

When a deal appears, fill these next to the wish list row (or on a second tab keyed to the same gift).

  • G: Sale price
  • H: Discount %: =IF(C2=0,"",ROUND((C2-G2)/C2*100,0))
  • I: Under max?: =IF(G2="","",IF(G2<=C2,"Buy","Skip"))
  • J: Bought? (Yes / No / Waiting)
  • K: Notes (ends at, coupon code, return window)

A simple buy rule

The Under max column is the whole strategy. If a deal is still above your max price, skip it even when the percent-off looks big. Percent-off ads often start from an inflated list price.

Separate Black Friday from the rest of Christmas

Keep a small holiday budget line for "sale weekend" so impulse buys do not eat the gift budget for people who are harder to shop for later. Move unused sale-weekend money back to the main gift budget on the Monday after.

Track returns and open-box risks

Add a return-by date column for anything you buy in a rush. Black Friday return windows are often shorter than normal, and open-box or warehouse deals may be final sale.

FAQ

Is there a free Black Friday gift budget spreadsheet?

Yes. The columns and formulas on this page are a working tracker you can copy into Google Sheets or Excel. The Christmas gift tracker template below already includes per-person budgets, a gift list, a holiday budget and an expense log you can reuse for sale weekend.

Should I shop Black Friday or wait for Cyber Monday?

It depends on the item. Track both against the same max price. Whichever hits your max first wins; if neither does, skip it.

How do I stop impulse buys?

Only buy items that already exist on the wish list with a max price. If it is not on the list, it waits until next week.
