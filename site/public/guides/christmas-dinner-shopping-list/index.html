Christmas dinner shopping list: what to buy, how much, and when to buy it

Short answer: Start from your menu and guest count, copy every ingredient from every recipe onto one list, then sort it by store section. Mark pantry goods, drinks and frozen items to buy one to two weeks ahead, and leave fresh produce, dairy and bread for two or three days before Christmas.

Count portions first

Write the guest count and any dietary needs at the top of the list. Scale each recipe to that number before you copy ingredients, so the quantities on the list are what you will actually cook.

Sort by store section

Grouping the list the way the store is laid out saves time and stops you missing things.

  • Produce: potatoes, carrots, sprouts, herbs, onions, garlic
  • Meat: the turkey, ham or roast, and bacon or sausages
  • Dairy and eggs: butter, cream, milk, cheese, eggs
  • Bakery: bread, rolls, pastry
  • Pantry: flour, stock, cranberry sauce, gravy, spices, oil
  • Frozen: desserts, ice cream, ice
  • Drinks: wine, juice, soda, sparkling water

Two shopping trips, not one

One to two weeks before, buy everything that keeps: pantry goods, drinks, frozen items, foil, roasting bags and napkins. Two or three days before, buy fresh produce, dairy and bread. Order the bird or roast in advance, and if it is frozen, allow time to thaw it in the fridge.

Check what you already have

Before the first trip, go through the list against your cupboards and fridge and cross off what you own. Spices, oil, flour and stock are often already there.

FAQ

How much turkey do I need per person?

A common guideline is about 1 to 1.5 pounds of whole turkey per person, more if you want leftovers.

When should I buy fresh vegetables for Christmas dinner?

Two or three days before is a good balance between freshness and avoiding the busiest last minute rush.
