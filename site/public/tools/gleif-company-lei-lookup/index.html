GLEIF Company LEI Lookup

Find any company LEI number by name, or look up an LEI, in the official GLEIF global registry. Get the legal name, status, jurisdiction, registration number, legal and HQ address, and direct and ultimate parent companies. For KYC, KYB, vendor checks and data enrichment.

Open on Apify: gouda_studios/gleif-company-lei-lookup

Use cases

  • KYB, onboarding and compliance checks
  • CRM and lead enrichment with verified legal names, addresses and parents
  • AI agents that must confirm a company exists and who owns it

Pricing

  • Actor start: $0.001 per run
  • $0.003 per company returned ($3.0 per 1,000)
  • No platform usage fee. Set maxResults to cap spend.

Input parameters

NameTypeDefaultDescription
companyNamesarray[]Legal names to look up, e.g. ["Apple Inc.","Siemens AG"]. Exact legal name match first, then fuzzy full-text.
leisarray[]Optional 20-character LEIs to fetch directly.
registrationNumbersarray[]Optional company-registry numbers as reported to GLEIF (registeredAs), e.g. a UK Companies House number.
countrystringOptional ISO 2-letter country of the legal address, e.g. US, GB, DE. Improves name matching.
activeOnlybooleantruetrue (default) = skip lapsed/inactive entities for name and registration searches.
maxMatchesPerNameinteger3How many candidate entities to return per name or registration number (1-50).
includeParentsbooleantruetrue (default) = add direct and ultimate parent (LEI, name, country) where reported.
maxResultsinteger100Maximum rows overall (1-5000). You pay per row: 100 rows = $0.30.
