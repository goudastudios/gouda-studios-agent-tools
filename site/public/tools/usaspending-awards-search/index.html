USAspending Federal Awards

Search USAspending.gov federal awards by keyword, recipient, agency, NAICS, state, date and amount. Get contracts and grants with amounts, recipients, awarding agency and performance dates as JSON. Track competitors, find who won contracts and build sales prospect lists.

Open on Apify: gouda_studios/usaspending-awards-search

Use cases

  • GovCon BD teams researching competitor wins and incumbents
  • Grant seekers and consultants checking who funds what
  • AI agents answering who won federal money for a topic, with sourced data

Pricing

  • Actor start: $0.001 per run
  • $0.0015 per award returned ($1.5 per 1,000)
  • No platform usage fee. Set maxResults to cap spend.

Input parameters

NameTypeDefaultDescription
keywordsarray[]Optional words to match in award descriptions, e.g. ["cybersecurity"].
recipientNamesarray[]Optional company or organization names (or UEIs), e.g. ["Booz Allen Hamilton"]. Find a competitor's federal wins.
awardingAgenciesarray[]Optional top-tier agency names exactly as USAspending lists them, e.g. ["Department of Defense","Department of Veterans Affairs"].
naicsCodesarray[]Optional NAICS codes (contracts only), e.g. ["541512"].
statesarray[]Optional 2-letter state codes, e.g. ["VA","TX"].
awardTypestring"contracts"contracts (default), idvs, grants, loans, direct_payments or other.
startDatestringYYYY-MM-DD. Defaults to 90 days ago. USAspending data starts 2007-10-01.
endDatestringYYYY-MM-DD. Defaults to today.
minAmountintegerOptional lower bound on award amount.
maxAmountintegerOptional upper bound on award amount.
onlyNewbooleanfalsetrue = skip awards delivered by an earlier run with the same filters.
maxResultsinteger100Maximum rows (1-10000). You pay per row: 100 rows = $0.15.
