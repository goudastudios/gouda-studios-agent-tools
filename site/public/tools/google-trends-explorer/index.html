Google Trends Scraper

Bulk Google Trends data for any keyword: interest over time, interest by region, top and rising related queries, plus today's trending searches. Any country, time range, category, or YouTube/News/Shopping search. Pay only per successful keyword; blocked terms are free.

Open on Apify: gouda_studios/google-trends-explorer

Use cases

  • SEO and content teams finding rising keywords and seasonality
  • Product and ecommerce researchers checking demand before they build or stock
  • Investors and analysts tracking search interest as a market signal
  • AI agents answering whether a topic is growing, and where, with real Google Trends data

Pricing

  • Actor start: $0.0005 per run
  • $0.004 per search term returned ($4.0 per 1,000)
  • No platform usage fee. Set maxResults to cap spend.

Input parameters

NameTypeDefaultDescription
searchTermsarrayKeywords or phrases. Each one is fetched separately and returns one result row.
geostring"US"ISO code like US, GB, DE, or US-CA. Leave empty for worldwide.
timeRangestring"today 12-m"How far back to look.
categoryinteger0Google Trends category number, 0 for all categories.
propertystring""Which Google search the trend data comes from.
interestOverTimebooleantrueTimeline of 0-100 interest values.
interestByRegionbooleantrue0-100 interest for each subregion.
relatedQueriesbooleantrueTop and rising related searches.
relatedTopicsbooleantrueTop and rising related topics (best effort: Google often withholds these from automated sessions, so the field may be empty).
trendingNowbooleanfalseAdds one row per currently trending search in the chosen country, with traffic and news links.
languagestring"en-US"Interface language for names, e.g. en-US, de, es.
maxTermsinteger100Stop after this many terms.
proxyConfigurationobjectOptional. Leave off to use our built-in residential proxy, included in the price.
